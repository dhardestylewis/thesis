 \multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{\textbf{Parcel \& Administrative Characteristics}}} \\[2pt]
 \textbf{Property Value} & Appraised and market value of the parcel and its improvements, from Travis County Appraisal District annual rolls \\
 \textbf{Parcel Size} & Lot size in acres, total building square footage, and the ratio of improvement value to land value \\
 \textbf{Improvement Scale} & Total improvement square footage and the developer's proposed architectural height in feet \\
 \textbf{Building Age} & Year the primary structure was built, and the derived age of the building at the time of application \\
 \textbf{Zoning Density} & The requested maximum floor-to-area ratio (FAR) and total permitted units under the target zoning classification \\
 \textbf{Land Use Type} & The parcel's current zoning and land use classification under Austin's Land Development Code \\
 \midrule
 \multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{\textbf{Neighborhood Characteristics}}} \\[2pt]
 \textbf{Racial Composition} & Share of nearby residents identifying as White, Hispanic, Black, or Asian, from ACS five-year estimates \\
 \textbf{Housing Tenure} & Share of occupied housing units that are owner-occupied versus renter-occupied \\
 \textbf{Income \& Rent} & Median household income, median gross rent, poverty rate, and median home value for the surrounding census tract \\
 \textbf{Prior Petition Activity} & Petition rate among zoning cases filed within one kilometer over the prior year, capturing neighborhood mobilization history \\
 \midrule
 \multicolumn{2}{l}{\textit{\textbf{Macroeconomic Context}}} \\[2pt]
 \textbf{Mortgage Rate} & The prevailing 30-year fixed mortgage rate, its 12-month lag, six-month momentum trend, and the change since the case's filing date \\
 \textbf{Capital Markets} & The 10-year Treasury yield and Federal Funds Rate, each with their 12-month lag and the change since filing \\
 \textbf{Local Labor Market} & The Austin-area unemployment rate, its 12-month lag, momentum, and the change since filing \\
