# Communication Style Guide: Dory & Thesis Updates

## Core Philosophy
**"Midway" Style**: Blend the user's technical precision with Dory's concise, action-oriented, "plain English" tone.
*   **Dory's Voice**: Functional, verb-first, low adjective usage, conversational but professional ("Nope, I have it under control", "Reach out by email").
*   **User's Voice**: Detailed, explanatory, focused on the "why" and "how".
*   **The Goal**: Clean, verifiable updates without "corporate fluff" or "strategic noise".

## ❌ Banned Vocabulary (The "Verbal Noise" List)
Avoid these terms which sound like AI or corporate strategy documents:
*   "Executed"
*   "Strategy" / "Strategic"
*   "Campaign"
*   "Retrieval"
*   "Pipeline" (unless referring to specifically code pipelines, but even then, simplify)
*   "Leveraging"
*   "Synergy"
*   "Unlock"
*   "High-value targets"
*   "Bypass"

## ✅ The "Midway" Dictionary (Use These Instead)

| Corporate/AI Speak | Dory/Midway Speak |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Executed a targeted retrieval strategy"** | **"Found alternative contacts"** or **"Looked up direct emails"** |
| **"Interview Campaign (Phase 2)"** | **"Interview Outreach"** or **"Follow-ups"** |
| **"Committee Outreach"** | **"Finding Readers"** or **"Reader Search"** |
| **"Data Acquisition Pipeline"** | **"Downloading Data"** or **"Data Collection"** |
| **"Drafted personalized invitations"** | **"Wrote emails to..."** |
| **"Scripted and queued..."** | **"Prepared drafts..."** |
| **"To unlock these targets..."** | **"In order to get a response..."** or **"To reach them..."** |

## Formatting Rules
1.  **Headers**: Use "Section A:", "Section B:", etc. (No all-caps SECTION).
2.  **Bullets**: Use `*` for all lists.
3.  **Tone**:
    *   **Past**: Simple past (Downloaded, Reviewed, Emailed).
    *   **Future**: Imperative (Draft, Send, Train).
    *   **Challenges**: Be honest and conversational.

## Example Rewrite

**Bad (Too Formal/AI):**
> *   **Interview Campaign (Phase 2)**: To unlock high-value targets who did not respond to the initial wave, I executed a targeted retrieval strategy to identify alternate routes.

**Good (Midway/Dory Style):**
> *   **Interview Follow-up**: 15 people from the Jan 23 outreach haven't responded (including Planning Directors). I am doing the following:
>     *   Found alternative email addresses or direct contacts for them.
>     *   Wrote new personalized emails to try again.
