About these estimates

Estimates come from a causal forest fitted to 6,637 Austin rezoning cases, most of them filed between 2000 and 2024, then applied to 271,567 parcels citywide. The citywide surface was computed on 11 May 2026.

These are average effects for parcels resembling each one, not forecasts for any particular application. Individual parcels carry wide uncertainty, and a single case can depart sharply from the average.

Roughly 3 percent of historical cases recorded organized petitioning, and the strongest opposition on record sits near 90 on this index. Settings above that read as extrapolation rather than evidence.

Texas Local Government Code chapter 211 lets owners of 20 percent of the land within 200 feet of a site force a three-quarters council vote. The opposition index here measures signature strength near the site, which is related to that rule but is not the statutory share itself.
