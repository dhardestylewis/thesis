# Structural Equation Models (SEMs)

Technical catalog of structural equations and their properties as defined in SCMs.

## Structural Definitions
*   **Definition 3.1.1 (Structural Equation):** $V_i = f_i(PA_i, U_i)$ where $PA_i$ are parents in the DAG and $U_i$ are exogenous variables.

## Property Log
*   **Modularity:** The ability to replace one equation without affecting the others (Intervention).
