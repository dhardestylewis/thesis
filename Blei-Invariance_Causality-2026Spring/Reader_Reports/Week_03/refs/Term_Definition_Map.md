# Term-Definition Map (Week 4 Rigor)

This document maps every term requested by the user to its precise definition and citation in the assigned literature.

## Core Concepts (Peters et al. 2017/2018)

*   **"Causal Mechanisms"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2018, p.19 (Principle 2.1)
    *   *Definition:* "The conditional distribution of each variable given its causes (i.e., its mechanism)."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Conditional Distributions"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2018, p.19
    *   *Definition:* "In the probabilistic case... the conditional distribution... (i.e., its mechanism)."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Autonomous"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2018, p.19
    *   *Definition:* "Autonomous modules that do not inform or influence each other."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Invariant"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2018, p.17
    *   *Definition:* "autonomous, modular, or invariant mechanisms."
    *   *Status:* Cited widely.

*   **"Shift"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2018, p.112 (Covariate Shift)
    *   *Definition:* "The influence of Y consists only in shifting the mean of X."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Conditional Mechanism"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2018, p.19
    *   *Definition:* Synonymous with "conditional distribution" in Principle 2.1.
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

## Invariant Causal Prediction (Peters et al. 2016)

*   **"ICP" (Invariant Causal Prediction)**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.947/953
    *   *Definition:* "Causal inference by using invariant prediction."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Plausible Causal Predictors"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.953 (Definition 1)
    *   *Definition:* "We call the variables S... plausible causal predictors under E if... H_{0,S}(E) is true."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary (Exact match).

*   **"Causal Predictors" / "Predictors"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.954
    *   *Definition:* "The estimator of the causal predictors will... not erroneously include non-causal predictors."
    *   *Status:* Covered under "Plausible Causal Predictors", but can separate.

*   **"Subsets"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.953
    *   *Definition:* "subsets S yielding an invariant..."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Yielding"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.953 (Definition 1 context)
    *   *Definition:* Operational word in defining the set S.
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Invariant across environments"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.953
    *   *Definition:* "assumed to stay identical across all environments."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Environment"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.949
    *   *Definition:* "different experimental conditions e... interpreted as... environments."
    *   *Status:* Cited widely.

*   **"Intersection"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.954 (Theorem 1)
    *   *Definition:* "The intersection... identifies causal ancestors."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Identify"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.954
    *   *Definition:* "Identifies causal ancestors."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.

*   **"Causal Ancestors"**
    *   *Source:* Peters 2016, p.954 (Theorem 1)
    *   *Definition:* "intersection ... identifies causal ancestors."
    *   *Status:* Cited in Summary.
