# Wu, Yin, Wang, Cunningham, and Blei (2025b) - Bayesian Invariance Modeling of Multi-Environment Data

**Source:** `References/Text_Converted/Wu_2025b_Bayesian_Invariance_Modeling.txt`

## Key Definitions

*   **Bayesian Invariant Prediction (BIP):** "BIP encodes the indices of invariant features as a latent variable and recover them by posterior inference" (p. 1).
*   **Contrast with Peters (2016):** "Previous methods have primarily tackled this problem through hypothesis testing... BIP... provides a model-based quantification of uncertainty" (p. 1).
*   **Invariance Assumption:** "The outcome, conditional on the invariant features, to be independent of the environment" (p. 2).
*   **Latent Variable:** "Crucially, the invariant feature set is treated as a latent variable" (p. 2).
